    Roadmap to the platinum

    Seven stages, built from the official trophy descriptions: the story in the order PlayStation lists it, the one-time moments flagged while you are still inside the story, then the sweeps — side stories, activities, collectibles, free-roam feats and money — and the 100% push last. Nothing outside the story stages can be missed, so if you catch the five flagged moments on the way through, the platinum is a matter of time, not luck.

    Missable trophies — the five to watch

    Flagged from the official descriptions alone: each of these five is tied to a moment the story stages once — an airport, a showroom, a tank, a first failure, the game's opening beach. Whether the game offers a chapter replay is not something the official list confirms, so the safe play is the same for all five: keep a manual save before big story missions, and take the moment when the story hands it to you. Everything not on this page can be earned in free roam afterwards.
